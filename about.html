About MDE Home Repair and Paint, Macclesfield NC

A family business out of Macclesfield, working Nash, Edgecombe, Wilson and Pitt Counties since October 2022.

  • BBB Accredited, A+ rated
  • Licensed and insured
  • Family owned and operated
  • Free written estimates
  • Serving Eastern NC since 2022

Devin

Devin runs MDE and he is the one who comes out and looks at your job. Not a salesman, not an estimator who has never held a saw. If he tells you the framing under your deck is sound, it is because he got under it.

He started the company in October 2022 doing exactly what the name says — repairs and painting. The work grew because customers kept asking. A porch repair turned into a porch rebuild. A ceiling stain turned into a roof. Somebody asked whether he did drywall, and it turned out he would rather hang and finish it himself than watch a sub do it badly. Now the company frames decks and porches, tears off and replaces roofs including flat commercial roofs, takes rooms to the joists and rebuilds them, and still does the repairs and the painting it started with.

We have not changed the name. But if you found us looking for a painter and you are standing in front of a deck that needs replacing, we can do that too.

Family run, and it shows in the small ways

You get the same person on the phone, on the estimate, and on the job. Nobody hands you off. If something goes wrong you call Devin directly and it gets sorted, because his name is on the truck and he lives twenty minutes from your house.

It also means we are honest about capacity. We would rather tell you we cannot start for three weeks than take a deposit and leave you waiting six.

BBB Accredited, A+ rated

We were accredited by the Better Business Bureau in November 2024 and carry an A+ rating. Accreditation is not automatic — the BBB looks at complaint history, how long you have been operating, licensing and advertising. It took two years of finished jobs before we applied for it.

We are licensed and insured, and you get a certificate of insurance before we start work, without having to ask twice.
