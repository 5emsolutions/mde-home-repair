What we run into on Bethel houses

Bethel is small and it is off the main run between the bigger towns, which means most people here have a story about a contractor who quoted a job and never came back. We are about half an hour away and we take work here on purpose, including work that is too small for a bigger outfit to bother with.

The housing here is older and it is mostly modest — frame houses, brick ranches, and a fair number of homes that have had a roof or an addition put on at some point by somebody who was working fast. Where two roof planes meet at an addition is where we find leaks more often than anywhere else in this town. Valley flashing done with caulk instead of metal will hold for years and then let go all at once.

North Pitt County also holds water. The land is flat, the drainage is slow, and after a heavy week the crawlspaces stay wet. That turns into soft subfloor, rot at the sill, and mildew on the north side of exteriors that will bleed straight through fresh paint if it is not killed first. We wash and treat before we paint here, every time.

Most common calls in Bethel

  • Leaks where an addition roof ties into the original roofline
  • Valley and step flashing done with caulk instead of metal
  • Wet crawlspaces turning into soft subfloor and rotted sill
  • Mildew on north-facing exteriors that has to be killed before paint
  • Small repairs other companies will not drive out for

Bethel questions

Will you actually come out to Bethel?

Yes. We are about thirty minutes away and we work here regularly. If we quote it, we schedule it, and if something moves we call you rather than leaving you wondering.

There is mildew on the side of my house. Is that a paint problem?

It is a wash-and-treat problem first. If you paint over mildew it comes back through the new coat within a year. We clean it, treat it, let it dry properly, and then paint.
