What we run into on Macclesfield houses

Getting a contractor to drive out to Macclesfield is the hard part for most people here. Crews out of Greenville or Rocky Mount will quote the job and then push it three times because it is the furthest stop on their board. We are not driving out to Macclesfield. We live here.

The work out here is farmhouse work and outbuilding work as much as it is house work. Older frame houses with metal roofs and wide porches. Shop buildings and barns with rusted-through panels and framing that has been repaired four times. Detached garages, well houses, and back steps that have been on the list for six years.

Farm country also means wind with nothing to slow it down. There is no tree line breaking it up across an open field, so what a storm does to a roof edge or a porch out here is different from what it does to the same roof in a subdivision. Ridge caps, drip edge and porch roof framing take the worst of it, and that is a lot of what we end up fixing.
