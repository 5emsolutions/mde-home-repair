What a commercial roof actually costs you when it fails

It is rarely the roof. It is the tenant whose inventory got wet, the ceiling tile budget, the lease conversation that gets harder every time it rains, and the deck you now have to replace because the insulation stayed saturated for two years.

A flat roof gives you a lot of warning before it goes. Ponding that does not drain in 48 hours, seams lifting at the laps, blisters, a membrane that has gone chalky and hard, interior stains that move. Caught at that stage you often have a maintenance scope and a few more budget cycles. Caught after the insulation is soaked, you have a tear-off.

We take core cuts and show you which one you have. That is a short visit and it costs you nothing.

What we do

  • Full tear-off and replacement. Down to the deck, deck repaired where needed, new insulation board, fully adhered or mechanically fastened EPDM, all new edge metal, flashing and drain details.
  • Leak diagnostics and repair. Finding where water actually enters, which is almost never above where it appears inside.
  • Preventive maintenance. Seam and flashing inspection, drain clearing, resealing penetrations, and a written condition report you can hand to an owner.
  • Roof-over evaluation. Sometimes a recover is legitimate and saves you a third of the cost. Sometimes the code and the wet insulation say no. We will tell you which.
  • Membranes we work with. EPDM primarily, plus TPO and modified bitumen, and metal roofs on older commercial buildings.

Budget numbers

Tear-off and new EPDM runs $8–$14 per square foot installed across this market. Drivers are membrane thickness, adhered versus mechanically fastened, insulation R-value, how much deck has to be replaced, and access.

What building owners ask us

Can you work while the building is occupied?

Yes, and most of what we do is occupied. We phase the roof so only the section we can dry in that day is ever open, and we sequence loud work — tear-off, fastening — around your tenants’ hours where the schedule allows it. We do not need interior access except at penetrations and interior drains, and we give notice before we need it.

How do I know whether to repair or replace?

Age, saturation and how many times it has already been patched. If the insulation under the membrane is wet, patching the top is money spent twice — the wet board keeps the deck damp and the R-value is gone anyway. We take core cuts and show you what is under there before you commit to either number.

What warranty comes with a new membrane?

Manufacturer warranties on EPDM typically run 10, 15 or 20 years depending on membrane thickness and whether it is fully adhered. That covers the material. Our workmanship is warranted separately and in writing. We will lay out what each one actually covers before you sign, because they are not the same thing and the difference matters at year seven.

Our capital budget is set for this year. Can you work to a date?

We can, and we would rather build to your fiscal calendar than push you into a bad month. If the roof can hold with a maintenance scope until next cycle we will tell you that and price the holding work. If it cannot, we will tell you that too, with photos.

Do you handle leaks on roofs you did not install?

Yes. Leak diagnostics is a good bit of our commercial work. Finding the entry point on a flat roof is its own skill — water travels along the deck and shows up thirty feet from where it got in.

Need a number for next year’s budget?

We will measure it, take core cuts, and give you a written scope and a real figure. No charge, and no pressure to spend it this cycle.
