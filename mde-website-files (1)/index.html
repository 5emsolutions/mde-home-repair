What we do

The name says repair and paint. The work is bigger than that.

We frame decks and porches, tear off and replace roofs including commercial flat roofs, take rooms down to the joists and rebuild them, and cut rot out of the side of your house before it becomes a framing problem. Painting is part of what we do, not all of it.

How it goes

Three steps, no runaround

1

You call or send photos

Tell us what is going on. Photos of the problem save everybody a trip and often get you a ballpark on the phone.

2

We come out and take a look

We walk the job with you, check anything that looks questionable, and get on the roof if there is one involved. The visit is free, with no pressure.

3

You get a written price

Line by line, with what is included and what is not. If we find something once we open it up, you hear about it before we do the work, not after.
